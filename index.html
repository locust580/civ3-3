How to Play

Overview

Lead your technological civilization from ancient automata to artificial general intelligence.

Core Loop

  • Build structures on hex tiles to generate resources each turn
  • Use Research Points in the Idea Lab to combine concepts and make discoveries
  • Expand territory, produce units, and wage war on rival civilizations
  • Research Branch Proficiency determines your success rate for cross-tradition synthesis

Resources

  • ⚡ Energy — powers buildings and units
  • 💎 Silicon — primary building material
  • 🔬 Research — fuel for concept synthesis
  • 💾 Data — advanced computation fuel
  • ⚙️ Compute — civilization processing power (score)
  • 🌍 Rare Earth — advanced components
  • ⚛️ Quantum — quantum-era technologies

Research Branches

Each concept belongs to a research branch. Your faction starts with high proficiency in its home branches but must work to reach distant traditions. Building branch-specific research buildings increases proficiency. Capturing enemy civilizations absorbs their expertise!

Winning

Discover AGI or Consciousness Upload (tech victory), or eliminate all other civilizations (military conquest).
